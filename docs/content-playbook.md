# Content Playbook

> Personal style guide based on posts that hit. Reference this before posting.

---

## Pre-Post Checklist

Before hitting send, check:

- [ ] Hook is controversial or creates curiosity?
- [ ] Uses "we" not "you" (includes reader)?
- [ ] Has a specific story, not just opinions?
- [ ] Punchline moment that names the absurdity?
- [ ] Receipts or proof (screenshots, data)?
- [ ] Ends with question or clear CTA?
- [ ] No typos?

---

## Winning Formats

### 1. The Frustrated Rant (High Engagement)

**When to use:** Calling out dumb behavior in your space

**Template:**
```
[Controversial truth about your space]

[Include yourself: "we" not "you"]

[Setup the logic simply]

"Simple, right?"

[Tell a specific story with stakes]

[Punchline that names the absurdity]

[Show receipts/data]

[Pause beat - "1 hour later?"]

[Reveal it got worse]

[End with frustrated question]
```

**Example (6.6K views, 114 likes, 46 comments):**
```
The biggest problem with web3 is how dumb we are.

We are our own worst enemy.

Our highest value metric in NFTs is floor price. So logically, the collective goal should be managing floor price so the project appears successful to outside buyers.

Simple, right?

My Legend of Breadio collection has had trading paused for over a month.

Since then, holders have been racing to undercut each other to sell and take profit.

The problem? They can't read.

The sales literally can't execute. Trading is paused.

The result? They're simultaneously kicking themselves in the nuts and complaining that their nuts hurt.

To highlight this, I opened trading for 10 minutes this morning.

Dumb participants out. Savvy participants in.

After closing secondary, the floor jumped from 0.00002 → 0.02 ETH.

1 hour later?

The same people are back racing to the bottom. Trying to get liquidity from a market that can't sell.

WTF are we doing here?
```

**Why it worked:**
- Self-deprecating hook that includes reader
- Universal frustration everyone feels but hasn't articulated
- Specific story with an experiment (not just opinion)
- Proof via screenshot
- Perfect pacing with pause beats
- Open question invites comments

## Signal Scoring

When editing, score each signal 1-10:

| Signal | What to check |
|--------|---------------|
| **Hook** | First line stops the scroll? |
| **Length** | Right length for the content? |
| **Engagement** | Question or CTA at end? |
| **Readability** | Good pacing, line breaks, no walls of text? |
| **CTA** | Clear action or response prompt? |

**Target:** 75+ total (avg 7.5+ per signal)

---

## Common Fixes

| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Hook is generic | Start with controversy, number, or question |
| Too long | Cut setup, get to story faster |
| No engagement | End with question, not statement |
| Feels preachy | Use "we" not "you", include yourself |
| No proof | Add screenshot, data, or specific example |
| Flat ending | Add pause beat before punchline |

---

## Quick Reference

**High engagement triggers:**
- Frustration everyone feels
- Specific numbers/data
- Screenshots as proof
- "1 hour later?" pause beats
- Self-deprecating honesty
- Open-ended questions

**Avoid:**
- "Due to overwhelming demand" (cliché)
- Generic AI-sounding phrases
- Ending with statements instead of questions
- Typos (kills credibility)
- Being preachy without including yourself
